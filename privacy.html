Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 19, 2026

About the application

AutoTube AI is a personally operated desktop application that assists with creating videos and, when authorized, uploading them to a YouTube channel.

Google account access

The application requests Google OAuth permissions to identify the authorized YouTube channel and upload videos. The account owner grants these permissions through Google's consent flow. AutoTube AI does not request or store the account owner's Google password.

Data used

The application may access the connected YouTube channel's identifier and name, and upload metadata such as titles, descriptions, visibility settings, and video files. OAuth credentials and related project files are stored on the owner's computer for operation of the personal application.

Storage and sharing

The website itself does not provide registration or collect visitor information through forms. The desktop application is not designed to sell Google user data or share OAuth credentials with advertisers. Authorized upload data is sent to Google's YouTube services when the owner initiates or schedules the relevant operation. Separate third-party services used to prepare source material may have their own policies.

Revocation and deletion

The account owner can revoke access in their Google Account settings under third-party connections. Locally saved OAuth authorization files can be deleted from the owner's computer. Removing authorization prevents further API access until permission is granted again.

Changes

This policy may be updated as the application changes. The current policy will be published at this address.

Contact

For questions about this personal application, use the developer support contact displayed in the application's Google OAuth consent screen.
